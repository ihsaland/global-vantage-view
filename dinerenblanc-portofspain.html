What is Dîner en Blanc?

A moment. A movement. A global tradition.

Thousands of guests dressed all in white arrive at a secret location for a chic picnic en masse — a global culinary phenomenon with joie de vivre and local flair.

Born in Paris in 1988 as an intimate gathering between friends, Dîner en Blanc has grown into a worldwide tradition celebrated in more than 80 cities across six continents. Each edition unites elegance, spontaneity, and community around a single evening — one shared table under an open sky, in a place revealed only at the last moment.

Guests bring their own tables, chairs, fine linens, and picnic baskets — turning a hidden public space into a candlelit banquet hall that appears for one night, then vanishes. It is a celebration of friendship, food, and the city itself.

The Seven Signature Moments

How the evening unfolds, moment by moment

01 · Le Rendez-Vous

The Gathering

Guests meet at designated points across the city with tables and picnic baskets in hand. Volunteers guide them toward the undisclosed destination.

02 · Arrival

The Reveal

Thousands converge on the secret location within twenty minutes, unfolding tables and place settings in unison across the space.

03 · The Napkin Wave

The Opening

At the appointed moment, guests wave white napkins in unison — the elegant signal that dinner has officially begun.

04 · The Diner

The Feast

An evening of shared plates, fine wine, and unhurried conversation with strangers who become friends by dessert.

05 · End of Dinner

The Illumination

As dinner concludes, the entire assembly lights the sky — sparklers and lanterns marking the transition from feast to celebration.

06 · Let's Dance

The Celebration

Live entertainment fills the night. Tables become a dancefloor. The city itself becomes the ballroom.

07 · Au Revoir

The Departure

Guests pack their tables and leave the space exactly as they found it — nothing left behind but the memory of a singular night.

The Port of Spain Edition

Trinidad & Tobago joins a global tradition

Trinidad & Tobago sits at the intersection of the Caribbean, Latin America, and the global diaspora — a natural setting for Dîner en Blanc's arrival. The Port of Spain edition brings together the region's most globally-connected professionals, entrepreneurs, creators, and cultural leaders for one night of shared elegance under the Caribbean sky.

The Port of Spain edition is produced and franchised by Global Vantage View LLC, bringing local craft to a global tradition — Caribbean warmth, Caribbean rhythm, Caribbean elegance, all delivered to the exacting standards of the international Dîner en Blanc network.
